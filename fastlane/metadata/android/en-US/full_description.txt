OpenFX is an open-source Android file manager. A single APK runs on phones, tablets and Android TV.

<b>File management</b>
• Browse, copy, move, delete, rename, search and bookmark
• Browse and extract archives; install APK and split APK bundles
• USB drives and SD cards, updated as they are plugged in or removed

<b>Phone ↔ TV transfer</b>
• Automatic discovery on the local network; pair by scanning the QR code on the TV
• Receivers require a 6-digit PIN and lock out for 60 seconds after 5 wrong tries
• Add a device by IP when the router blocks broadcasts

<b>Network shares (SMB / NAS)</b>
• Saved servers with passwords encrypted by Android Keystore
• Copy and move between the device and the NAS; stream video and music with seeking

<b>Encrypted vault</b>
• AES-256-GCM encryption of files, names and folders
• Fingerprint unlock, auto-lock, screenshot blocking, encrypted backup and restore

<b>Also</b>
• Every screen works with a TV remote
• Traditional Chinese, English and Simplified Chinese, switchable inside the app

Permissions: "All files access" is used to manage files; "Query all packages" is used to send installed apps to another device.
